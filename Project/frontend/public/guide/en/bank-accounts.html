Bank accounts

Bank accounts are the source of the balances you record in the Current situation chapter, and they decide whether a cut's total goes up or down. This chapter covers the account list, creating and editing an account, and soft-deleting and restoring one.

The account list

Every account shows its alias, currency, type, and display order. Type is either Adds — the account contributes positively to the cut's total, for a checking or savings account — or Subtracts, for a liability such as a credit card balance. Display order controls the row order everywhere the account appears, including the Current situation form. A budget with no accounts yet shows an empty-state message instead of an empty table.

Creating an account

Only admins and the owner see the "New Account" button. The create form asks for an alias, a currency, and whether the account adds to or subtracts from the total; the positive/negative toggle defaults to adding. The alias must be unique within the budget — reusing one is rejected with a dedicated "an account with this alias already exists" message rather than a generic save-failed one.

Editing an account

Editing lets you rename the alias, flip the adds/subtracts type, or change the display order. The currency is locked once the account is created — it cannot be changed afterward, only replaced by deleting the account and creating a new one in the target currency.

Deleting and restoring an account

Deleting an account is a soft delete: the account disappears from the default list but its history is preserved. A confirmation dialog names the account by alias before anything happens. To find a deleted account again, turn on "Show deleted" above the table — deleted rows appear dimmed with a "deleted" badge and a Restore action in place of edit/delete. Restoring reinstates the account exactly as it was, including its display order.
