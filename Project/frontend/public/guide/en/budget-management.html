Budgets

A budget is the top-level container for everything else in MyBudget: cycles, categories, accounts, and members all belong to exactly one budget. This chapter covers listing your budgets, creating a new one, deleting and restoring a budget, and inviting someone else to join.

Your budget list

Signing in takes you to /budgets, listing every budget you belong to along with your role in each one. If you belong to exactly one active budget, MyBudget skips this screen and drops you straight into it — the list only appears when you have more than one, or when you navigate back to it deliberately.

Creating a budget

"New Budget" opens a form asking only for a name. Budget names must be unique per account — reusing one you already have is rejected rather than creating a duplicate.

Creating a budget makes you its owner and navigates straight into it — an empty cycle list, ready for you to add your first cycle.

Renaming and deleting a budget

Owners and admins can rename a budget with a double-click on its name in the list, or the pencil icon. Only the owner can delete a budget — deletion is a soft delete behind a confirmation dialog, so the budget disappears from the active list but is not destroyed.

Turning on "Show deleted" reveals soft-deleted budgets with a "Deleted" badge and a Restore action, so an accidental delete is always recoverable.

Inviting someone to a budget

Owners and admins can invite another person from the list's invite icon. The invite form asks for an email address and a role (Admin, Operator, or Read-only — a budget cannot be shared with someone as a second Owner). MyBudget rejects the invite up front if that person is already a member, or if the inviter is not permitted to invite for that budget.

The invitee receives an email with an accept link. If they already have a MyBudget session, accepting adds the budget to their list immediately and shows a success message.

An expired link, an already-used link, or a link opened by the wrong email address shows an error instead of a crash — the invitee can ask the budget's owner or an admin to send a new one. The Members chapter covers how roles are managed once someone has joined.
