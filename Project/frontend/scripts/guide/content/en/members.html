Members

The Members tab manages who has access to a budget and what they can do in it: viewing the member list, changing roles, removing access, restoring it, and sending new invitations. It is text-only in this guide — there are no screenshots for this chapter.

Who can manage members

The Members tab itself only appears in a budget's tab bar for that budget's admins and owner — an operator or read-only member never sees it.

Inside the tab, whether a role-change control, a "Remove" button, or a "Restore" button appears for a given row follows one rule: it renders only when all of the following are true — you are an admin or the owner, the row is not your own, the row is not the owner's, and — unless you are the owner — the row is not another admin's. The same rule applies to soft-deleted rows, so a role you cannot change is also a role you cannot restore. The server enforces this independently; the interface only hides what would otherwise be rejected.

Viewing the member list

The list shows Name, Email, Role, and Joined date for every active member of the budget. The owner's own row never appears here — ownership is not something this tab manages or transfers. A "Show deleted" toggle reveals members who were previously removed, listed alongside the active ones at reduced opacity.

Changing a member's role

When you can act on a member's row, their role shows as a dropdown — Admin, Operator, or Read Only — instead of a plain badge. Picking a different value saves immediately: a success message confirms the change, or an error message explains why it was rejected.

Removing access

"Remove" opens a confirmation dialog warning that the member will lose access to this budget. Confirming removes them from the active list. Nothing about their history is deleted — a removed member can always be restored later with the role they had before.

Restoring access

Turning on "Show deleted" reveals removed members. For any row you can act on, a "Restore" button replaces "Remove" and reinstates that person with their prior role.

Inviting someone

Sending an invitation is not done from the Members tab — look for the "Invite user" icon next to a budget's name on the budget selection screen, visible to that budget's admins and owner. It opens a form asking for the invitee's email address and a role, defaulting to Operator (Owner is not an assignable role). The server rejects the request up front if that email is already a member of the budget, if Owner was somehow selected, or if the inviter does not have permission to invite for that budget — each case shows its own message.

This chapter stops once the invitation is sent. What happens when the invitee opens the invitation link — accepting it and joining the budget — is covered in the Budgets chapter.
